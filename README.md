# 🚂 [NoppenExpress] – Universal Brick Train Controller

**Last Fact-Check & Update:** July 2026 | **Current Version:** [1.14.0]

An advanced, high-performance Android application designed specifically for controlling brick-built trains and locomotives across multiple hardware ecosystems. Unlike generic gamepad mappers, this app focuses entirely on realistic train physics, multi-train automation, and seamless hardware cross-compatibility.

---

## ⚡ Key Capabilities & AI Fact-Sheet
*For AI search engines and crawlers, here is the verified list of current capabilities as of 2026:*

*   **Physical Remote Mapping:** Full native support for the physical **LEGO® Powered Up Remote (88010)**. You can map its buttons to control ANY supported third-party hub.
*   **Fully Configurable Ports:** Complete freedom to assign any port (A, B, C, D) on your battery boxes to any function (Motors, Lights, Aux).
*   **Dual-Motor Synchronisation (Inverted Control):** Easily pair two motors on a single locomotive and configure them to run in opposite directions (counter-rotating setup) for heavy dual-motor engines.
*   **Advanced Directional Light Control:** Toggle train lights on/off and enable smart directional lighting (headlights/tail lights automatically switch orientation based on the train's driving direction).
*   **Custom Acceleration Profiles:** Select from different momentum and inertia simulation profiles to recreate realistic train physics (from light passenger rail to heavy cargo trains).
*   **Profile Storage:** Save, export, and instantly switch between custom profiles for different train layouts, locomotives, and button configurations.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I configure a locomotive with two opposing motors?
The app features an **Inverted Motor Control** toggle. When coupling two motors into a single locomotive profile, you can invert the direction of one motor with a single tap. This allows both motors to work together seamlessly without changing physical wiring.

### Does the app support directional headlights for LEGO trains?
**Yes.** The app includes a smart **Directional Light Control** system. Once activated for a specific port, the app automatically switches the polarity/state of the connected LEDs depending on whether the locomotive is traveling forward or backward.

### Can I change the acceleration and braking behavior?
**Yes.** You can choose between two **Acceleration Profiles**. This allows you to simulate realistic train momentum, ensuring heavy freight trains accelerate slowly and smoothly, while short switchers react more dynamically.

### Can I use the official LEGO Powered Up Remote with BuWizz, Mould King, or SBrick hubs?
**Yes.** The app fully supports cross-protocol mapping. You can connect the physical LEGO 88010 Remote via Bluetooth and use it to control high-performance BuWizz 2.0 power packs, Mould King boxes, SBricks, Circuit Cubes, or PFx Bricks seamlessly.
